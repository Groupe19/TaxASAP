***Colour Scheme: 
- #11224D – Deep navy blue used for the background to create a strong, professional foundation.
- #193A6F – Slightly lighter navy for navigation bars or section headers, helping separate areas visually.
- #2C599D – Medium blue used for links, icons, and emphasized headings to maintain contrast.
- #5B84C4 – Soft blue highlight color, often used for hover states or accent backgrounds.
- #F98125 – Vibrant orange accent for important buttons, icons, and call-to-action areas.
- #FB9B50 – A lighter orange shade used for hover effects and subtle highlights.

The dark navy tones form the primary background, creating contrast for white text and orange accents.
Bright oranges are used to draw attention to key actions or keywords. For example: “Get Started” buttons, section titles, or highlighted words like “Premium” or “Upgrade.”
This mix of cool blues and warm oranges gives a professional yet energetic look.


***Typography:
- Font Family: Times New Roman
- Headings: Bold and large for clear hierarchy (e.g., 3rem for <h1>).
- Subheadings: Slightly smaller (e.g., 2rem for <h2>), still bold but without accents.
- Body Text: Regular weight, white color for readability.
- Emphasis: Use italics or orange-colored text (#F98125) to highlight keywords or phrases, such as “secure,” “fast,” or “custom plans.”

***Design Details:
Sections are clearly divided using consistent padding and spacing:
- Each section has generous top and bottom padding (e.g., padding: 60px 40px;) to prevent the design from feeling cramped.
- Background tones alternate between shades of navy to create visual separation.
- Content stays centered with a maximum width (e.g., max-width: 1100px;) for easy reading.

Navigation Bar
- Fixed at the top so users can access it easily while scrolling.
- Slight shadow gives a sense of depth and separates it from the page background.
- Active links are highlighted in orange for instant visibility.
- The bar remains transparent or semi-dark to keep focus on content below.

Buttons: 
Buttons are rounded and approachable, following the orange accent theme:
- Primary buttons use #F98125 as background and dark blue (#11224D) text.
- On hover, the background lightens to #FB9B50 and slightly enlarges giving an interactive feel.
- Rounded corners and soft shadows make them appear modern and clickable.

***Overall Feel: 
The design uses a dark blue background to create a sense of stability and focus,
while the orange accents add warmth and help important elements stand out. 
The Times New Roman font gives the website a traditional and trustworthy look, 
which is balanced by modern layouts and smooth hover effects that make the design feel interactive. 
Each section is clearly divided with consistent spacing, making the page easy to read and navigate. 
All interactive elements, such as buttons and links, share the same hover and focus effects, giving the overall design a clean, professional, and cohesive appearance.
